The right podcast music comes down to three decisions: what your show's personality actually is, where you place music across intro, background, transitions, and outro, and whether each of those spots calls for instrumental or vocal tracks. Nail those and the music supports the show without anyone noticing it's there. Get them wrong, and listeners clock it before you've said a word.
