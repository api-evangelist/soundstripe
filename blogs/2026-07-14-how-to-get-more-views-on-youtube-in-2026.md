To get more views on YouTube, the two signals that matter most are click-through rate (CTR, whether your title and thumbnail convince someone to click) and average view duration , which tells the YouTube algorithm whether viewers actually stayed after clicking. Both signals directly control how aggressively YouTube promotes your video to new audiences.
