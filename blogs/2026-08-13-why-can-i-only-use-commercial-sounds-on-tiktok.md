TikTok Business Accounts see only the Commercial Music Library in the in-app editor, because the platform's personal-use music licenses do not extend to commercial or promotional content. The CML holds over one million pre-cleared tracks and is the default sound source for Business Account organic posts. Businesses can also upload videos containing music they have licensed themselves, after completing TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation.
