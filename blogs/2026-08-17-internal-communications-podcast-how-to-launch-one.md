An internal communications podcast is a private, audio-only show a company records for its own employees, not the public. It works alongside the channels you already have (the newsletter, the all-hands recap, the Slack update) except people listen instead of read, usually on a commute or in the gap between meetings. Access stays locked down through a password, a permissioned intranet link, or a private RSS feed, so it never shows up in a podcast app next to anyone else's show.
