The best Musicbed alternatives in 2026 are Soundstripe, Artlist, Epidemic Sound, Track Club by Marmoset, Storyblocks, Audiio, AudioJungle, and Lickd. These platforms all cover the core need for licensed music in video, brand, and commercial work, but they differ significantly on catalog size, pricing model, and which publishing platforms their licenses protect.
