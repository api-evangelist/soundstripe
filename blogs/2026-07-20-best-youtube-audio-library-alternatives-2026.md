The YouTube Audio Library is a free collection of royalty-free music and sound effects inside YouTube Studio , but its catalog holds several thousand tracks and its license covers YouTube use only. For creators looking for free music for YouTube videos with broader rights coverage, or royalty-free music for YouTube and every platform where they distribute content, the Audio Library hits its ceiling fast.
