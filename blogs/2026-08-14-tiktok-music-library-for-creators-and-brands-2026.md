TikTok maintains two separate music libraries: the General Sound Library for personal creators, and the Commercial Music Library (CML) for business accounts. The CML holds over 1 million pre-cleared, royalty-free tracks and sound effects available free to any TikTok Business Account. The detail most guides skip: the CML license covers TikTok content only.
