A podcast's sonic identity is everything a listener hears that tells them, without looking at a screen, which show they're on: the intro and theme music, the stings between segments, the outro, and how consistent the host's voice and mix sound from one episode to the next. Sonic branding for podcasts means choosing those elements on purpose and keeping them consistent, instead of picking one good intro track and calling the job done.
