The safest way to avoid a copyright strike on YouTube is to only use content you have explicit permission to use. Three strikes within 90 days means permanent channel termination, all your videos disappear, and you are banned from creating new accounts. Each strike expires 90 days after it is issued, provided you complete YouTube Copyright School (which you only have to do once).
