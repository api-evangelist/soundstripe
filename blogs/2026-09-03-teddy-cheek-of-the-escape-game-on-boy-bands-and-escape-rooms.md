Teddy Cheek is VP of retail operations at Great Big Game Show, the live game show experience built by the team behind The Escape Game. He spent close to a decade running marketing at The Escape Game as it went from five locations to more than 60, and before that he marketed recording artists in Nashville, some of whom he can name and some of whom he can't.
