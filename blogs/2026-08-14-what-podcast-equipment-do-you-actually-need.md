You need four things to record a podcast that sounds good: a microphone, headphones, something to record into, and a quiet-enough room. Everything else (interfaces, boom arms, mixers, cameras) is an upgrade you add once you know what your show actually needs. Most people can start for under $150 and add gear as they go.
