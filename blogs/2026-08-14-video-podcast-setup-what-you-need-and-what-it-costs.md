A video podcast setup starts with the same audio gear as any podcast (a decent microphone, headphones, and a treated room) and adds a camera, lighting, and recording software on top. Get the audio chain right first. The camera and lights matter, but a clean mic in a dead room will always beat a great camera pointed at bad sound.
