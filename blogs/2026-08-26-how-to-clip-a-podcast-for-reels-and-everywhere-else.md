You clip a podcast by pulling one strong, self-contained moment out of a full episode and reshaping it for vertical video: crop it to 9:16, trim it to somewhere in the 30 to 60 second range, add captions, and post it. That's the whole job. The part people get stuck on isn't the technical piece, it's picking the right fifteen seconds.
