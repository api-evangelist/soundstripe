Starting a faceless YouTube channel is a highly scalable way to build an audience and earn income without appearing on camera. Finance and tech channels in this format earn $8 to $18 per 1,000 views , with top creators reporting six-figure annual income from content that never shows a single face. At a typical $3 to $5 RPM, reaching $2,000 a month from ads requires between 400,000 and 650,000 monthly views.
