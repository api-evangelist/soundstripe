J.R. McNeely has mixed more than 300 albums across nearly three decades, for Underoath, Relient K, TobyMac, DC Talk and a long list of others. He has a Grammy on a shelf, a nomination he cares about more than the win, and a stack of gold and platinum plaques he only hung up because his wife wanted them out of her closet.
