Aaron Sprinkle produced MXPX's debut as a teenager, spent the mid-2000s making records that a Buzzfeed writer would later call the best emo albums of 2005, and has written 648 songs for the Soundstripe catalog under more than a dozen names. He has never taken a music lesson in his life.
