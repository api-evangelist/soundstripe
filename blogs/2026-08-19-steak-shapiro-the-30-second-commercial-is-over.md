Steak Shapiro mailed cassette tapes to 14 program directors he had never met, got one call back, and has been in Atlanta ever since. He was fired inside a year, so he wrote a business plan and bought a radio station instead. He ran 790 The Zone for 17 years, sold it, got fired again, and built a content studio that now makes work for Coca-Cola, Delta and Home Depot.
